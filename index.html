Both platforms are simulated in your browser, so it's safe to click anything. The sync rules are the ones used in the real build: matching by Shopify ID, duplicate blocking, loop protection and retries.

How it works

Small, event-driven, easy to maintain

A lightweight sync service sits between the two platforms. Each side tells it the moment something changes.

Shopify Signed webhooks for customers, orders and inventory

Salesforce Change Data Capture on Contact, Order and Product
No duplicatesEvery Salesforce record stores its Shopify ID, so changes update the right record.
Repeats ignoredEach event ID is recorded once. A webhook sent twice is only processed once.
No update loopsChanges made by the sync itself are recognised and skipped on the way back.
Automatic retriesIf a side is down, changes wait in a queue with back-off, then catch up.
SecureOnly signed Shopify webhooks are accepted. Salesforce access uses a limited integration user.
Clear logsEvery step is logged with time, record and result, so problems are easy to trace.
