    02How it works

    Two teams, one set of records.

    A small sync service sits between the two systems. Each side tells it the moment something changes, and it writes the change to the other side.

    1. 01
      Shopify speaks first. Signed webhooks arrive the moment a customer, order or stock level changes. Anything without a valid signature is turned away.
    2. 02
      Matched, never duplicated. Every Salesforce record keeps its Shopify ID, so a change updates the right record. A webhook sent twice is only processed once.
    3. 03
      Salesforce answers back. Change Data Capture reports edits made by your team, and the sync writes them to Shopify. Its own writes are recognised, so nothing loops.
    4. 04
      Nothing gets lost. If either side is down, changes wait in a retry queue and go through once it's back. Every step is logged with a reason.
